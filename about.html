About

Our mission

Flagship Growth’s mission is to scale brands efficiently on the digital shelf through expert strategy and fast, high-impact deliverables, backed by the day-to-day account management to see it through. Your digital shelf performance shapes the voice of your customer: your content, ratings, search rank and in-stock rate decide what shoppers see, and what they say about you afterward. We work across Amazon, Walmart and TikTok Shop as an extension of your team, not a vendor you have to manage.

Our story

Flagship Growth was founded by Joey Giazzon and Vinny Alvarez, two operators with 30+ years of combined operator experience. Before Flagship, Vinny grew an Amazon division from $90M to $500M+ in annual managed revenue as VP of Amazon Account Strategy, leading 10+ account strategists across Grocery, Home & Household, Sports & Outdoors and Toys. Joey launched private-label brands in sporting goods, then grew 10+ client accounts from $30M to $50M+ in annual revenue as a Senior Account Strategist at a 150+ person agency. They started Flagship to give brands the senior team they would want running their own business: the people who build the plan run the account every day.
