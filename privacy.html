Privacy Policy.

Last updated October 26, 2026.

Flagship Growth ("Flagship Growth," "we," "us," or "our") operates flagshipgrowth.com (the "Site"). We also operate Flagship Insights, an analytics app for Amazon sellers at insights.flagshipgrowth.com (the "App"). This policy explains what information we collect from Site visitors and App users, how we use it, who we share it with, how we protect and delete it, and the choices you have. How the App handles data from Amazon is covered in Flagship Insights and Amazon seller data.

Information we collect

Information you provide directly. When you submit our contact form, we collect your name, email address, company name, monthly revenue range, and any message you include. When you book a call through our embedded scheduling widget, Calendly collects the information needed to confirm that booking (see Calendly's own privacy policy for how it handles that data).

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, we automatically receive certain information when you visit the Site, including your IP address, browser and device type, pages viewed, referring URL, and general location. We collect this through Google Analytics and Meta Pixel, described below.

Cookies and tracking technologies

Strictly necessary. The Site works without any cookies. We store one small value in your browser (fg_consent, in local storage) to remember your cookie choice, so we don't ask again on every page.

Analytics and advertising (optional, off until you accept). If you click Accept all in our cookie banner, we turn on:

  • Google Analytics (GA4) — website usage and traffic analytics. Cookies such as _ga and _ga_*, kept up to 2 years.
  • Meta Pixel — measures the performance of our Meta (Facebook/Instagram) advertising and which visits lead to inquiries. Cookies such as _fbp, kept up to 90 days.

Until you accept, Google Analytics runs in Google's Consent Mode with storage denied (no analytics or advertising cookies are set) and the Meta Pixel does not send data. If you click Reject non-essential, they stay off.

Changing your choice. Use Your privacy choices at the bottom of any page to accept or withdraw consent at any time. You can also clear the Site's data in your browser, opt out of personalized advertising through Meta's ad preferences, or use the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Global Privacy Control. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal, we treat it as a request to opt out: analytics and advertising tools stay off and the banner won't turn them on.

How we use your information

We use the information we collect to: respond to your inquiry and evaluate whether our services are a fit for your brand; operate, maintain, and improve the Site; and measure and improve the performance of our marketing and advertising, including which campaigns and content lead to genuine inquiries.

We do not sell your personal information for money. Under some U.S. state laws (for example California's), letting the Meta Pixel measure our ads may count as "sharing" personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. It only happens if you accept cookies, and you can opt out at any time with Your privacy choices or a Global Privacy Control signal.

Who we share information with

We share information with the third-party service providers who help us run the Site and our business, including: Netlify (website hosting), FormSubmit.co (delivers contact form submissions to our email), Calendly (call scheduling), Google (analytics), and Meta (advertising measurement). Each of these providers processes data under their own privacy policies. We may also disclose information if required by law.

Data storage and security

The Site is served over HTTPS with a valid SSL certificate. We do not collect or store payment or financial account information on the Site. Contact form submissions are delivered directly to our email; we do not maintain a separate marketing database beyond what's needed to follow up on an inquiry.

Data retention

We retain the information you submit for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your inquiry and for legitimate business record-keeping, or until you request deletion.

Flagship Insights and Amazon seller data

This section covers Flagship Insights, our analytics app at insights.flagshipgrowth.com, and the data it receives from Amazon when a seller authorizes it. It follows Amazon's Data Protection Policy and Acceptable Use Policy for apps that use the Selling Partner API.

What we collect

  • App users: name, email address, company and role of each person invited to the App, and basic sign-in records (time and device type), so we can secure the account.
  • Amazon data, only after the seller authorizes it: sales and traffic reports, Brand Analytics search reports, Fulfilled by Amazon inventory, catalog and listing details, product prices and featured-offer status, and the marketplaces the account sells in.
  • Data the seller's team enters: product costs, forecasts, notes and edits.

The App does not request or store buyer personal information such as names, addresses, phone numbers or email addresses, and it does not access payment or bank details.

How we use it

Only to provide the App to the seller who authorized it: to build that seller's dashboard, reports, forecasts and recommendations, to support the seller's team, and to deliver the management services the seller has engaged us for. We do not aggregate one seller's data with another's, provide it to other sellers, use it to produce information about Amazon's business, sell it, or use it for advertising.

Who can see it and who we share it with

A seller's data is visible only to the users that seller invites and to the Flagship Growth staff assigned to that seller's account. We share it only with the service providers that run the App for us, each bound to keep it confidential and secure: Netlify (hosting), Neon (database hosting, through Netlify), Clerk (user sign-in; receives app users' names and emails, not Amazon data), and Anthropic (the AI model that drafts the weekly recommendations from that seller's figures; Anthropic does not use this data to train its models). We may also disclose data if the law requires it. We check that each provider's security standards are at least as strict as our own.

How we store and protect it

  • All data is encrypted in transit with TLS 1.2 or higher and stored with encryption at rest.
  • The authorization tokens Amazon issues are additionally encrypted with AES-256-GCM, using a key stored separately from the database.
  • Every page that shows account data requires sign-in, each seller's data is kept separate and labeled with its source, and staff access follows least privilege, is reviewed regularly and is removed within 24 hours when someone leaves or changes role.
  • If we detect a security incident affecting Amazon data, we notify Amazon (security@amazon.com) and the affected seller within 24 hours.

How long we keep it and how it is deleted

We keep Amazon data only while the seller's authorization is active and only as long as needed to provide the App. We permanently delete a seller's Amazon data, including all copies, within 30 days after the earliest of: the seller revoking the App in Seller Central, the seller's account with us ending, Amazon asking us to delete it, or our access ending for any other reason. The only exception is a specific record the law requires us to keep, which we keep only for that purpose. A seller can ask us to delete its data at any time by emailing joey@flagshipgrowth.com.

Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information we hold about you by emailing joey@flagshipgrowth.com. Depending on your location, you may have additional rights under applicable regional regulations (such as the GDPR or CCPA); we will honor valid requests to the extent required by law.

Children's privacy

The Site is intended for business use and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or how we handle your information? Email joey@flagshipgrowth.com.
