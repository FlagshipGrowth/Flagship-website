Amazon account management: common questions

01What is Amazon account management?+

It is the day-to-day running of a brand’s Amazon business by an outside team: keeping the account healthy, resolving cases with Amazon, protecting the Buy Box and pricing, running advertising, keeping listings and content strong, planning inventory, and reporting on results. At Flagship Growth one senior account manager coordinates all of it.

02Do you manage Seller Central and Vendor Central?+

Yes. We run Seller Central (3P) and Vendor Central (1P) accounts. For Vendor Central brands that includes shortage and chargeback disputes to recover money Amazon owes you.

03How often do you report?+

Every Monday you get a performance report in your inbox, plus a weekly business review. Sales, ads, search and inventory are also live in your Flagship Insights dashboard, so you see the same numbers we do.

04How does pricing work?+

We don’t sell a fixed package. Every engagement is quoted individually based on account size, channel mix, and how much creative and media support you need. Book a call and we’ll scope real numbers for your account.

05How do you start?+

With the Free Growth Audit: a review of your ads, creative, listings and account health before anything changes, and a 30-60-90 day plan with the fixes ranked by what they’re worth. Then we connect your accounts, set up your dashboard and move to a weekly rhythm.

06Can you take over from another agency or an in-house team?+

Yes. Most of our clients come to us from another agency or from managing their account in-house. The first step is always a full audit of the existing account before anything changes.

07Do you also manage Walmart and TikTok Shop?+

Yes. The same team runs Walmart, TikTok Shop and Target, plus Meta Ads for external traffic, so promotions, inventory and ads stay in sync. See all services.
