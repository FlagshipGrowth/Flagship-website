Accessibility statement.

Last updated October 24, 2026.

Flagship Growth wants everyone to be able to use flagshipgrowth.com, including people who use screen readers, keyboard navigation, magnification or other assistive technology.

Our standard

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA, the standard referenced by most accessibility laws and courts.

What we've done

  • A "Skip to content" link at the top of every page, and visible focus outlines for keyboard users.
  • Page structure built on headings and landmarks (header, navigation, main content, footer) so screen readers can move around quickly.
  • Text alternatives for meaningful images; decorative images are hidden from screen readers.
  • Labelled form fields on the contact form, and a mobile menu button that announces whether it's open.
  • Animations that respect your device's "reduce motion" setting.
  • A cookie banner that works with a keyboard and screen reader, and honors Global Privacy Control.

Known limitations

  • Some case-study and blog images contain text from brand creative. We describe what matters in the surrounding text, but not every word in an image.
  • The call-booking widget is provided by Calendly. Its accessibility is managed by Calendly; if it gives you trouble, email us and we'll book a time for you.

Feedback and help

If anything on this site is hard to use, or you need information in another format, email joey@flagshipgrowth.com with the page and what happened. We aim to reply within two business days and to fix confirmed issues promptly.

How we check

We review the site with keyboard-only navigation, a screen reader and automated checks when we make changes, and fix issues we find.
