Brand Guidelines

Everything needed to apply the Flagship Growth identity consistently — logo files, color, type, and voice. Last updated 2026.

01 — Logo

One lockup. Two backgrounds.

The primary logo is the mark plus wordmark, set in Space Grotesk. Use the light-background version on paper/cream or white surfaces, and the dark-background version on ink or photography.

Icon mark

Three ascending bars topped with a flag — growth, with a flagship flying at the peak. Use the standalone mark for favicons, social avatars, and anywhere the full wordmark won't fit.

Clearspace & minimum size

Keep clear space around the logo equal to the height of the flag pennant on all sides — don't let text, edges, or other logos crowd it. Never scale the full lockup smaller than 100px wide.

Don't

Recolor the logo outside the approved palette.

Stretch, skew, or distort the proportions.

Add drop shadows, glows, or bevel effects.

Place the light-bg version on low-contrast or busy backgrounds.

02 — Color

A small, deliberate palette.

Ink and paper carry the page. Electric blue is the one accent — used for links, CTAs, and emphasis. Acid lime is reserved for small, rare highlights (the flag, a stat callout). If everything is an accent, nothing is.

03 — Typography

Space Grotesk for voice. Inter for clarity.

Space Grotesk carries headlines, buttons, stats, and labels — it's the personality. Inter handles body copy and anything that needs to be read at length. Never substitute a system font for either.

Aa Bb Cc Space Grotesk / 700 / Headlines, hero type
Aa Bb Cc 123 Space Grotesk / 600 / Subheads, stat numbers, buttons
Aa Bb Cc 123 Space Grotesk / 500 / Labels, eyebrows, tags
Full-service management, creative, and media for CPG brands growing on Amazon, TikTok Shop, and Walmart. Inter / 400 / Body copy
Senior strategists, sharp creative, and account management that answers the phone. Inter / 600 / Emphasis, nav, UI labels

04 — Voice

Direct. Specific. No filler.

We write like an operator, not a marketer. Every claim is backed by a real number where one exists — no vague "results-driven" language. Confidence comes from specificity, not adjectives.

Say this

"+97% YoY unit growth."

"We're the team you forget to worry about."

"Earned Amazon's Choice on the brand's highest-demand keyword."

Not this

"Massive growth results."

"We're passionate about your success."

"Best-in-class optimization solutions."
