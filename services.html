Common questions

01 What does Flagship Growth charge? +

We don't sell a fixed package — every engagement is quoted individually based on account size, channel mix, and how much creative and media support you need. Book a call and we'll scope real numbers for your account.

02 Which marketplaces do you manage? +

Amazon, TikTok Shop, Walmart, and Target, plus Meta Ads management for driving qualified external traffic into your listings.

03 How fast will I see results? +

It depends on the starting point — a Buy Box or conversion fix can move within weeks, while full account turnarounds (like our Rationale Brewing or Hapad case studies) compound over several months. We'll give you a realistic timeline after the audit, not a guess upfront.

Yes. Most of our clients come to us from another agency or from managing their account in-house. The first step is always a full audit of the existing account before anything changes.

Yes — we're an Amazon SPN (Solutions Provider Network) Verified Partner and a TikTok Shop Certified Agency.

A full review of your ads, creative, listings and account health on the channels you sell on, and a 30-60-90 day plan with the fixes ranked by what they’re worth. No pressure, no lock-in pitch.

07 What size brand do you work with? +

We work with CPG brands from under $50K to $5M+ in monthly revenue — the right fit is less about size and more about whether you're ready to invest in growth across channels.
